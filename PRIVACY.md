# Privacy policy: leavemealone

Last updated: 21 November 2026 · Published by Alicia & Antony Co.

leavemealone is an unofficial browser extension for the CUE student portal. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to CHRIST (Deemed to be University) or the ESPro / CUE portal.

## What it reads
Only pages on `https://cue.christuniversity.in/`, and only while you have them open: your attendance table, absence lists, timetable, and the name and class shown in the portal header (used to keep separate profiles when more than one student uses the same browser).

## What it stores, and where
Everything is saved in your own browser with `chrome.storage.local`:
- attendance numbers per course, absent hours and how you marked them, hours you typed in;
- your timetable and your "Put a bunk" plan;
- your settings (minimum %, ESE target, alerts, and so on);
- for profiles, only the first name, as a label.

## What it sends
Nothing. The extension makes no network requests other than loading its own bundled files (its stylesheet and font). There is no account, no analytics, no tracking, no ads, and no data is sold or shared with anyone, including us. We never see your data.

## Notifications
The optional daily alert is generated on your device from the data above. Turn it off in the popup's Settings.

## Your control
- Settings → Erase deletes everything the extension saved.
- Settings → Export / Import saves or restores a backup file that only you hold.
- Uninstalling the extension removes all of its data.

## Contributions
The welcome page shows a UPI ID and QR code for optional contributions. Paying happens entirely in your own UPI app; the extension doesn't see or record it.
